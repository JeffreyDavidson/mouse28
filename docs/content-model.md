# Mouse28 Context

Mouse28 is a Disney parks podcast and blog from Jeffrey and Cassie's perspective as parents raising Viola, an autistic nonverbal child. Use these terms when planning content, features, and issues.

## Language

**Trip report**
A firsthand recap of a Disney visit, including what worked, what was hard, and practical takeaways.
_Avoid_: review when the piece is personal experience rather than evaluating a venue/product.

**Accessibility resource**
A practical guide for families navigating Disney accessibility, sensory needs, DAS, quiet areas, planning, and park logistics.
_Avoid_: medical advice, therapy advice.

**Sensory break**
A planned or spontaneous pause to reduce overwhelm during a park day.
_Avoid_: meltdown prevention as a promise.

**DAS**
Disney's Disability Access Service. Treat policy details as version-sensitive and verify current wording before publishing guidance.
_Avoid_: guaranteed accommodation.

**Guide**
A durable, structured resource page meant to stay useful over time.
_Avoid_: post when the content should live in the guides/accessibility hub.

**Post**
A blog article, trip report, news reaction, recap, or family-life piece.
_Avoid_: guide when the content is timely or personal.

**Episode**
A podcast episode with audio, show notes, distribution metadata, and optional related posts.
_Avoid_: podcast when referring to one installment.

**Community story**
A contributed family experience or reader-submitted story.
_Avoid_: testimonial unless it is explicitly praising Mouse28.

**Host voice**
Jeffrey and Cassie speaking candidly as parents, not as medical or Disney policy authorities.
_Avoid_: expert voice unless citing a qualified source.

## Relationships

- Episodes can have related Posts and show notes.
- Guides form the accessibility hub and should be maintained as policy changes.
- Trip reports can feed both Posts and Episode topics.
- Community stories support the mission but need privacy-sensitive handling.

## Flagged Ambiguities

- "Accessibility" can mean Disney policy, sensory planning, mobility, food allergies, or family logistics. Name the specific concern.
- "Review" can imply a consumer rating. Use **trip report** or **food review** when appropriate.
- "Advice" should be framed as lived experience unless backed by authoritative sources.
